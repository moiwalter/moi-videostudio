# Paper Collage

Use real photographs, authentic documents, logos and real footage. Keep provenance and a frozen local original; preserve the colors and facts of the resource. No fabricated “news”, fake letters, invented product photos or generic cable footage to stand for a data center. Choose a resource for the narrated idea, not just the keyword.

Build the actual opening first. The speaker's presence is an episode choice. A recognizable face, document or logo can support the hook; it must not obscure the speaker or subtitle band. Establish the palette from this client before extending the look.

A cutout needs a role after entering: follow the narration through comparison, replacement, collision, reveal, scale change or a cut to real video. Show conflict with actual opposing movement or comparison; a static row of logos does not communicate a battle. The metaphor must not assert an unsupported factual relationship.

Inspect the actual candidate in its intended crop and at mobile size. Chips must read as chips; a power constraint needs appropriate generation/grid imagery. Check foreground/background hierarchy and accidental duplicate people. Use the speaker inside the collage when it helps continuity, without imposing a fixed number of takeovers.

Per scene record: interval; narrated words; visual purpose; speaker treatment; real assets and crop; entrance/action/exit; captions/emphasis; layers; transition/joins; provenance; review evidence; actual decision. Use resources.template.json for provenance. Build one concrete scene, preview natively with voice, repair, and record approval before propagating the approved direction.

Do not inherit Christian's purple, AI spelling, caption gaps, 19 scenes, specific people, or timing into another episode. Do not turn the reference project into a source-replacement script. Reuse the style principles and primitives; derive the story anew.

Engine, source, brand, variant and next action live in pipeline.json. Shared stage gates: ../../PIPELINE-WORKFLOW.md.
